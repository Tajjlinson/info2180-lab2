My First Night in Survival Mode

September 27, 2026 by Tajaun Tomlinson

Spawning into a brand new world is exciting at first, until the clock starts ticking the moment you appear. I spent the first few minutes jabbing at trees, in order to gather wood, and crafting a basic set of tools. By the time the sun started setting, I had a wooden pickaxe, a few pieces of cobblestone, and absolutely no shelter.

I dug into the side of a hill and sealed my self in with dirt blocks just as the ombies started appearing. It wasn't the cleanest of living conditions, but it kept me alive. A single torch lit up my tiny cave while I crafted a furnace and planned my next move. Note to self: Always build shelter before dusk.

Building My First Redstone Door

October 3, 2026 by Tajaun Tomlinson

Redstone confused me. I had no idea what it did and so it sat in my inventory for a while, until i decided to learn it. I startyed with something simple: a 2 x 2 piston door that opens when you press 'j' button. After watching a lot of youtube tutorials, I finally got the hang of it and built my self a working piston door.

The trick to it was understanding how the redstone signals travelled and how repeaters add delay. Once that clicked, everything else made sense. next order of business, is building a hidden entrance behind a waterfall for my base. Wish me luck!
